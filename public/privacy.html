Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Chania AI is a job-matching service for job seekers in Kenya. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Chania AI (“we”, “us”) runs the service at chaniajobsai.co.ke. For anything in this policy, contact us at chaniajobsai@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Your career profile: full name, education level, institution, course and dates, skills, work experience (current and past jobs), certifications, projects, achievements, languages and phone number.
  • Documents you add: files you attach when applying for a job, such as a CV.
  • Chat messages you send to the Chania AI assistant.
  • Technical data: a random device identifier stored in your browser, and a notification token if you turn on push notifications.
  • Payments: when you pay the contribution by M-Pesa, your phone number is used to send the payment prompt. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN.

How we use it

  • To build your profile and match it to job opportunities.
  • To alert you about new matches by push notification or SMS.
  • To write a CV and cover letter tailored to a job you choose to apply for.
  • To answer your questions in chat.
  • To process the contribution you choose to pay.
  • To let you get your profile back after you clear your browser or change phones, using your Google account.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Google user data

We use your Google name and email only to identify your account and restore your profile. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it except with the service providers listed below that help us run the service. Chania AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

AI processing

To match jobs, write documents and answer questions, parts of your profile and messages are processed by AI services, including Cloudflare Workers AI and Google Gemini. They process the data on our behalf to provide the service.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers: Cloudflare (hosting, database and AI), Google (sign-in, Gemini and push notifications through Firebase), Africa’s Talking (SMS alerts) and Safaricom M-Pesa (payments).
  • Employers: when you choose to apply for a job, the documents and details you submit go to that employer or its application channel.
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile for as long as you use Chania AI. Job matches are short-lived and refresh regularly. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete your profile and linked information.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or give you a copy. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email chaniajobsai@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will reply within 30 days. You can also turn off push notifications in your browser settings at any time.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored with Cloudflare. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

Children

Chania AI is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: chaniajobsai@gmail.com.
